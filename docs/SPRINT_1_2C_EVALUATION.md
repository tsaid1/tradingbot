# Sprint 1.2C — Real-corpus evaluation of the TKL interpreter

**Status:** evaluation tooling complete and exercised on synthetic fixtures; the baseline on the 120-post development dataset is **pending the Source Agent's adapter**, which is not in the repository yet (`research/sprint1.2-source-corpus` is identical to `main`). Mock-only: the runner adds no validator and no journal; it replays through the Sprint 1.1 coordinator. Reports contain aggregates, IDs and failure codes only. No subscriber text is ever written, printed or committed.

## 1. Tooling

| Piece | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `src/tradingbot/evaluation.py` | Label schema (`LabeledPost` / `LabeledAction`), chronological runner `evaluate()`, action matching, metrics, failure ranking, recommendations, golden replays (`GoldenReplay`, `run_golden`) |
| `scripts/evaluate_corpus.py` | CLI: `--synthetic`, `--posts` (JSONL of `SourcePost` dicts or G0 records), or `--adapter module:historical_inputs`; `--labels`; `--golden DIR`; writes `report.json` and `report.md`. Refuses any path containing `holdout` unless `--allow-holdout` is passed |
| `fixtures/eval_synthetic/` | 33 invented posts, 36 labeled actions, four golden replays; regenerated by `scripts/make_synthetic_eval_fixtures.py` |
| `tests/test_evaluation.py`, `tests/test_chronology_and_export.py` | 25 tests: scoring math, matching, executable-candidate definition, false-executable and missed-instruction reporting, linkage rule, golden mismatch detection, CLI run with a no-text check, holdout guard, availability-time chronology, export/manifest/checksum intake, typed reader arguments |

**Labels.** One JSONL line per post: `source_id`, `revision`, `actions[]` with `message_class`, `action`, `tense`, `asset`, `side`, `prices` (role → value), `close_fraction`, `links_to` (the `source_id` of the opening post) and `executable` (an explicit instruction the follower should act on now). An empty `prices` dict means "not labeled for prices", not "no prices". Private labels live under the git-ignored `data/eval/`.

**Metrics.**
- *Post level:* precision/recall of "an executable instruction is present" and of "any instruction is present" (instruction classes: OPEN, ADD, REDUCE, CLOSE, MODIFY, EXIT_ANNOUNCEMENT, CONDITIONAL, CANCEL).
- *Action level:* predicted clauses are paired with labeled actions greedily (same asset and action, then same asset and class, then same class); precision/recall overall and per action.
- *Temporal classification:* message-class and tense accuracy on matched pairs, with a confusion table.
- *Price roles:* per role, correct / wrong / missing / extra against labeled values (decimal equality).
- *Position linkage:* for executable follow-ups, whether the predicted idea reference points at the labeled opening post.
- *False executable candidates:* predictions that would reach the gateway as current, fill-capable, unambiguous candidates from an eligible post, where the label says nothing should execute. The one metric that must be zero.
- *Missed instructions:* labeled executable actions with no executable prediction, split into phrasing misses, semantic misses (recognised but not executable) and ambiguity holds (fail-closed by design).
- *Failure ranking:* every discrepancy is tagged with a failure kind; kinds are ranked by severity × frequency (severity 5 = could place a wrong fictional order, 1 = cosmetic).

**Chronology.** Posts are interpreted in **availability order** against the point-in-time context: a live post at its receipt time, an edited revision at its edit time (`revised_at`), everything else at publication. `SourcePost.available_at` is the single ordering and clock key used by `coerce_source_posts`, `replay_posts`, `evaluate` and `run_golden`, so an edit is processed after every post that appeared between the original and the edit, and the replay clock never runs backwards. Unlabeled posts still shape the context.

## 2. Synthetic results (interpreter as merged at 93e3ac1, unmodified)

33 posts, 36 labeled actions. The set plants four phrasings outside the lexicon (labeled executable) and several distractors carrying tickers and prices.

| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| Post level, executable instruction present | precision 100.0%, recall 78.9% (15 TP, 0 FP, 4 FN) |
| Post level, any instruction present | precision 100.0%, recall 83.3% |
| Action level, all actions | precision 84.2%, recall 88.9%, F1 86.5% (32 TP, 6 FP, 4 FN; the FPs are COMMENTARY fallbacks for the four misses plus two harmless fragments) |
| Temporal classification (matched) | class 100.0%, tense 100.0% |
| Price roles | ENTRY 9/11 correct, STOP 8/11, TARGET 2/3, EXIT 10/12, TRIGGER 2/2, P&L 3/4 (missing values belong to the four missed posts; wrong values to one post, see below) |
| Position linkage (executable follow-ups) | 11/11 correct |
| False executable candidates | **0** |
| Missed instructions | 4, all phrasing |
| Golden replays | TLT lifecycle, S&P reversal, staged partial exits, natural-gas conditional exit: **all pass** |

### Failure ranking (synthetic)

| Rank | Failure | Count | Severity | Finding |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | MISSED_INSTRUCTION_PHRASING | 4 | 3 | "Initiated a long in … this morning" (past-tense entry announcement), "Moving our $GLD stop up to …" (asset between verb and object), "Out of $SLV here at …" (terse exit), "Cutting our … long loose at …" (idiom). All fall back to COMMENTARY, so they fail closed; one cascades into a later close that cannot link |
| 2 | PRICE_ROLE_WRONG | 2 | 4 | One post, two defects in the interpreter: (a) a price immediately followed by a comma loses its decimals (`$31.20,` → `31`) because the price regex's trailing lookahead excludes commas and backtracks; (b) the role is taken from any keyword within 28 characters before the number, so in "stop $29.50, target $36" the target inherits the earlier "stop" |
| 3 | PRICE_ROLE_MISSING | 1 | 2 | Consequence of (b): the TARGET role is never assigned |

The two price-role defects are real interpreter bugs found by the evaluation. They are **deliberately not fixed in this PR** so the development-set baseline measures the interpreter exactly as merged. Recommended fixes, each a one-line change in `tkl_interpreter.py`: make the trailing lookahead `(?!\.?\d)` instead of `(?![\d,])`, and assign the role from the keyword closest to the number instead of the first keyword found in the window. Both come with the synthetic fixture `e04` as a regression case.

### LLM-assisted second path: not justified by the measured data

All four misses are lexicon gaps that a handful of deterministic patterns close at zero cost and with no new failure surface, and no miss is a context or reasoning failure. The verdict stands until the development-set baseline shows misses that are not phrasing gaps (for instance, instructions whose meaning depends on the quoted post or on a chart). The decision rule proposed for that baseline: an LLM second pass is justified only if, after the measured phrasing gaps are added to the lexicon, the remaining executable-instruction recall is below 90% **and** the residual misses are semantic rather than lexical. A second pass would also have to send paid text to an external model, which remains blocked until the publisher's permission is recorded (spec D-07).

## 3. Intake contract for the development export

The Source Agent delivers the 120-post development split as a JSONL export (lines are `SourcePost` dicts, schema 1.2, or raw G0 records) plus a manifest sidecar:

```json
{"split": "development", "post_count": 120, "sha256": "<sha-256 of the export file>",
 "labels_sha256": "<sha-256 of the labels file>", "source": "<adapter version or note>"}
```

Unknown manifest keys are ignored. `load_export()` refuses the file when the digest does not match (manifest and/or `--sha256`), when the split is `holdout` (unless `--allow-holdout`, final evaluation only), when the declared count differs from the file, or when no checksum is available at all. The labels file is checked against `labels_sha256` or `--labels-sha256`. The report's `input` block records the verified digests and split.

Alternatively the CLI calls the adapter directly: `--adapter package.module:historical_inputs` with any number of `--reader-arg key=value` (ISO timestamps and integers are typed before the call), so the adapter's own reader signature is honoured without a shim; `split=holdout` is refused there too. `tests/test_source_agent_compat.py::check_historical_inputs` remains the consumer-side contract check.

## 4. Running the development-set baseline (when the adapter lands)

```bash
# verified export + manifest, labels checked against the manifest's labels_sha256
python scripts/evaluate_corpus.py --export data/eval/dev_export.jsonl --manifest data/eval/dev_export.manifest.json \
    --labels data/eval/dev_labels.jsonl --expected-author <account id> \
    --golden data/eval/golden --out data/eval/results_dev
# or straight from the adapter with its own reader arguments
python scripts/evaluate_corpus.py --adapter <package.module>:historical_inputs \
    --reader-arg split=development --reader-arg start=2025-01-01T00:00:00Z --reader-arg end=2026-11-01T00:00:00Z \
    --labels data/eval/dev_labels.jsonl --labels-sha256 <hex> --expected-author <account id> \
    --golden data/eval/golden --out data/eval/results_dev
```

- Interpreter untouched for the first run; the report is the baseline.
- The four chronological golden replays against real posts are JSON specs in the private `data/eval/golden/` that reference `source_id`s (no inline text); expected statuses and orders come from the product owner's reading of each episode. The synthetic specs show the format.
- The 365-post holdout is never read: the CLI refuses any path containing `holdout`, and `--allow-holdout` is for the final evaluation only.
- Everything under `data/` is git-ignored; only `report.md` aggregates (IDs and codes) may be shared.

## 5. Known limits of the evaluation itself

- Matching is greedy; a post with several same-asset actions of the same type (two reduces in one post) could pair the wrong instances, affecting price-role and fraction scores but not counts.
- Executability is scored from the interpreter's output plus post eligibility; it does not re-run the gateway's market checks (collar, staleness, notional), which depend on quotes that labels do not carry. Golden replays cover that path.
- Labels are single-annotator until the holdout is labeled independently.
